Our Story

Covenant Crest Group Ltd (Company No. 16528951) was founded after one observation repeated across every sector we work in: agencies over-promise, under-deliver, and disappear when things go wrong. We were built to be the exception.

“Built on promise” is not a slogan. It is the operating principle behind every placement we make, every load we move, and every trade relationship we broker. When Covenant Crest makes a commitment, the founder's name is behind it — not a client services team reading from a script.

Headquartered in Telford, Shropshire, we serve clients and candidates across the UK, and work with importers, exporters and buyers in international markets — with particular depth in UK–India trade routes.

The Six Principles We Don’t Negotiate On

We put our values in writing and hold ourselves publicly accountable to them. Judge us against these.

01
Accountability
We own every outcome. When something goes wrong — and in fast-moving sectors it sometimes does — we fix it without excuses and without billing you for the privilege.
02
Transparency
No hidden fees, no vague terms, no surprises. Clients and candidates always know exactly what they’re getting and what it costs.
03
Speed Without Shortcuts
2-hour response guarantees. Same-day deployments. Quote turnarounds before lunch. Speed is non-negotiable — but so is the compliance check that precedes every action.
04
Compliance Before Revenue
Every candidate RTW and DBS-verified. Every shipment documented. Every contract auditable. We have turned work away rather than place an unvetted candidate. We always will.
05
Relationships Over Transactions
You speak to the same person every time. We know your site, your compliance requirements, and your preferred shift patterns — because we wrote them down the first time you told us.
06
Earned Growth
We grow by doing the work, not by winning the pitch. Every new client has come through a referral or a reputation — not a cold outreach campaign or a discounted rate.
