Sector Expertise

Specialist Supply. One Trusted Partner.

Our consultants come from the industries we serve. We speak your language and understand the specific compliance requirements of your sector.

Care & Healthcare

CQC Regulation 19 compliant vetting. Enhanced DBS on all candidates. NMC Pin verification for nursing staff. Skills for Care aligned training. Safeguarding Level 2 minimum. Residential and domiciliary supply.

Security

All operatives SIA licence-verified with expiry monitoring. Vetted to BS 7858:2019. Counter-terrorism awareness (ACT) trained staff available. Proactive licence renewal alerts to clients.

Warehouse & Logistics

GLAA licence application in progress. FLT licence verified for all forklift operatives. CSCS card checks. RIDDOR awareness. Site induction compliance management. PPE and manual handling records maintained.

Construction & Trades

CSCS-certified labourers, machine operators (CPCS/NPORS), and skilled trades for residential, commercial, and infrastructure sites. Right-to-work and qualifications verified.
